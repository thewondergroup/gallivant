The character

A table for the occasion

Candlelight, white tablecloths and food for sharing – inspired by the Paris brasseries that people in the know frequent.

We’re the restaurant locals pick when there’s something to celebrate. Order to share – a whole roast fish or a cut of beef – and finish with our signature tarte fine aux pommes, flambéed in Calvados at your table.

By day it’s relaxed and full of light: slow weekend lunches and a Sunday roast with a silver gravy jug that doesn’t leave the table.

In season this autumn

What’s good right now

The produce we’re most excited about this autumn, from the coast and the Marsh around us. It shapes the menu week by week.

01Essex
Maldon oysters

From the Blackwater estuary. Back with the “R” months – cold water makes them firm, briny and sweet.

02Rye Bay
Skate and brown shrimps

Landed by Rye’s day boats. Delicate, sweet skate wings, and tiny brown shrimps from the shallow, sandy bay.

03Hastings
Blue lobster

From Hastings’ beach-launched fleet. Inky blue in the pot, sweet and dense now the water’s at its warmest.

04The Marsh
Romney Marsh lamb

Grazed on the salt marsh next door, which gives the meat its gentle, savoury depth. Fuller in flavour by autumn.

05The woods
Girolles and field mushrooms

Apricot-scented girolles from the woods, and field mushrooms that spring up in the meadows after late-summer rain.

06Autumn veg
Beetroot and endive

Beetroot turns sweeter with the first cold nights; crisp, bitter-edged endive is just coming into its own.

The kitchen

A team to make you smile

Matthew Harris

Executive Chef

More than thirty years at the stove, twenty as Head Chef at Bibendum on the Fulham Road under his mentor Simon Hopkinson. He brought his French-led cooking to the coast – steak with Roquefort butter and the tarte fine aux pommes made the journey with him.

Gianmarco Abramo

Head Chef

Trained at Sketch, Joël Robuchon and Aquavit before head chef roles at The Rabbit and Cora Pearl. Runs the kitchen day to day alongside Matthew, and the seasonal specials that follow what’s good that week.

Good to know

FAQs

Anything else? Call Harry’s on 01797 330 955 or email hello@harryscambersands.co.uk.

Can I bring children?

Children aged 6 and over are welcome at lunch, and 12 and over at dinner. There’s no children’s menu, and we don’t serve half portions.

Are dogs allowed?

Dogs are welcome in the Sitting Room and the Bar at lunchtime, but not in the dining room or in the evening.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Yes, happily. There’s a vegetarian menu, and the kitchen can adapt dishes for allergies and other needs. Just let us know when you book.

Can I book for a large group?

For bigger celebrations, The Cabin seats up to 18 for lunch or dinner, with its own menus. Make a private dining enquiry.
