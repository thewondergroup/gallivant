Christmas and New Year 2026

Come home to the festive season by the sea

We all love Christmas. But somewhere around the third reheating of the turkey, we all start dreaming of getting away. Which is rather the point of the Gallivant over the holidays.

Pull on your favourite jumper, climb the dunes and walk along an empty beach. As evening falls, a cocktail in Siggy's, then a long dinner at Harry's, a nightcap by the fire and off to bed. Whether it's Christmas itself, the quiet days in between or the last night of the year, spend some of it with us.
