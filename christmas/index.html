Christmas

A happy Christmas by the sea. Long beach walks, a roaring log fire, long lunches and candlelit dinners at Harry’s. It’s the season to be jolly.

You’re invited

We all love Christmas. But somewhere around the third reheating of the turkey, we all start dreaming of getting away. Which is rather the point of the Gallivant over the holiday season.

Pull on your favourite jumper, climb the dunes and walk along our deserted beach. As evening falls, a cocktail in Siggy’s and then a truly epic dinner in Harry’s alongside a bottle you probably shouldn’t have ordered, then backgammon, a nightcap and off to bed.

With no children on their iPads in sight, Christmas at the Gallivant is both the happiest and most relaxing place to capture the true spirit of Christmas. Whatever your plans, spend some of them with us.

Christmas at the Gallivant

Four magical days. You are invited to come and spend Christmas by the sea. Crisp mornings on our sandy beach, walks across the salts to the cobbled streets of Rye, long afternoon naps. Your favourite cocktail in Siggy’s before candlelit, long lingering dinners in Harry’s.

Wednesday

A warm welcome. Arrive to warm mulled wine, mince pies fresh out of the oven and brandy butter. Settle in to your new home by the sea: our team are here to make it a magical Christmas.

  1. AfternoonWelcome mulled wine, warm mince pies and brandy butter, from 4pm
  2. EveningDinner in Harry’s

Christmas Eve

Beach walks and the cobbled streets of Rye. A leisurely breakfast, time for walks on the beach and an afternoon in Rye browsing the artisan shops and antiques. Later, welcome back for Christmas cake before a lingering dinner in Harry’s.

  1. MorningGallivant breakfast
  2. AfternoonChristmas cake and tea
  3. EveningChristmas Eve dinner in Harry’s

Christmas Day

Champagne and a traditional feast. Start with a champagne breakfast alongside a present or two. With no turkey to roast, the morning is yours. A glass of champagne and canapés, a traditional Christmas lunch (with crackers, of course), then an afternoon of leisure: Christmas cake, board games by the fire and a light supper whenever you fancy.

  1. MorningChampagne breakfast
  2. MiddayChampagne and canapés
  3. LunchChristmas lunch in Harry’s
  4. AfternoonChristmas cake, tea and board games
  5. EveningChristmas supper tray, to enjoy wherever you like

Boxing Day

Fond farewells. For those inclined, a gentle morning yoga class to shake off the feasting, then one last Gallivant breakfast before saying goodbye and heading home.

  1. MorningGentle yoga class for everyone
  2. BreakfastGallivant breakfast
  3. Mid morningFarewells and departures, before 11am

Escape to the Gallivant over the holidays

We might well be the ultimate home from home to escape to over the Christmas and New Year holidays. If you need to get away from it all in search of the carefree happiness that spending a day or two by the sea can bring, we’re here for you.

We offer the simple pleasures that make a trip away over the holidays feel like a proper escape. Deeply comfortable beds, exquisite food and drink, long walks on a sandy beach. A team to make you smile, a roaring log fire and not a young child in sight.

Happy holidays at the Gallivant.
