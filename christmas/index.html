You're invited

Our Christmas house party by the sea – crisp beach walks, a roaring log fire and long, candlelit dinners at Harry’s.

Imagine crisp December days filled with invigorating walks along the beach, followed by cosy evenings of games and conversation beside the fire. As twilight falls, craft cocktails await, then intimate dinners in our acclaimed restaurant.

We’ve attended to every detail, so all that’s left for you to do is arrive, unwind and enjoy it.

Your Christmas, day by day

Four days, beautifully planned

Every meal and festive moment is included. Join in as much or as little as you like.

A warm welcome

Arrive to freshly baked mince pies, brandy butter and tea in the Sitting Room, then settle in for dinner at Harry’s.

Beach walks and the snug

A leisurely breakfast, time for long walks on the beach and a lazy afternoon in the snug, then our special Christmas Eve menu.

  1. MorningGallivant breakfast

Christmas Day

Champagne and the feast

Start with a champagne breakfast, then champagne and canapés before our three-course Christmas lunch. Board games by the fire, and a supper tray whenever you’re ready.
