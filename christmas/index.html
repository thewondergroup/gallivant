Christmas Gallivanting

A happy Christmas by the sea. Long beach walks, a roaring log fire, long lunches and candlelit dinners at Harry’s.

Book a stayChristmas menus

You’re invited

We all love Christmas. But somewhere around the third reheating of the turkey, and the family row about which channel to watch, we all start dreaming of getting away.

Which is rather the point of the Gallivant over the holiday season.

Pull on your favourite jumper, climb the dunes and walk along our deserted beach till you feel your head switch off. As evening falls Harry’s is there for the kind of food you linger over with the bottle you probably shouldn’t have ordered, then backgammon, a nightcap and off to bed.

Come for a night or two or stay for Christmas or New Year. However you spend the holidays, spend some of them here with us.
