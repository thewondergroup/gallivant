Your private house by the sea

The party everyone talks about for a year. For a night or two, the Gallivant stops being a hotel and becomes your house: Siggy’s Bar, the Sitting Room, the Snug, the Terrace, the garden and Harry’s restaurant. Our team will know everyone’s name by the second round.

Welcome drinks in the dunes as the sun goes down. A long dinner at Harry’s. A DJ in Siggy’s until late. Then everyone walks to bed instead of hunting for a taxi, and the morning after starts with breakfast and a swim.

  • Milestone birthdays
  • Anniversaries
  • Reunions and family gatherings
  • Engagement and post-registry-office parties

Questions

How many guests can you host?

Up to 70 for a party, with 40 for dinner at Harry’s. Twenty bedrooms, each for two adults, with cottages nearby for more.

Is it exclusive use?

Yes. The whole house is yours, with no other guests.

Can we have music late?

Music runs to 11pm as standard, and we can apply for a later licence for one-off occasions. Ask Holly.

Can children come?

We welcome guests aged 12 and over.

Can you arrange transport from London?

Yes. A return coach transfer from London is around £1,500.

Can we visit first?

Of course. Holly will arrange it.
