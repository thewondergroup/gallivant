How it works

  1. 1

    A new door at midday

    From Tuesday 1 to Thursday 10 December, a different Gallivant gift goes live at 12pm each day.

  2. 2

    24 hours only

    Each gift keeps its special price until midday the next day. Then the door closes and the next one opens.

  3. 3

    Follow along

    We reveal every gift on Instagram stories at midday too, so follow @thegallivant and you won’t miss a door.
