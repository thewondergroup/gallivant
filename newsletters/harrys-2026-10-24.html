This weekend at Harry’s · 24 & 25 October

A table for
the occasion

Some lunches are just lunch. Others turn into the best part of the weekend: a birthday, old friends round one table, the first properly cold Sunday of the year, or simply the people you haven’t sat down with for far too long.

That’s what Harry’s is for. Candlelight, white tablecloths and food made for sharing, cooked by Matthew and the kitchen from what the coast and Romney Marsh are giving us right now. A table for the occasion, whatever the occasion happens to be.

And on Sunday the clocks go back, so there’s an extra hour to linger over pudding.
