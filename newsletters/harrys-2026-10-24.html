Sunday lunch, then open all week

A table for
the occasion

Some of my happiest memories start with a long lunch, the kind that turns into an afternoon you’re still talking about on Monday.

Wait for a break in the weather and walk the beach before or after (ideally both). Then come in, cheeks pink from the sea air, to a busy room and a proper French brasserie lunch from Matthew, Gianmarco and the team.

And on Sunday the clocks go back. An extra hour. I’d suggest spending it on pudding.

Harry
