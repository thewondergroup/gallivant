Head Chef

Meet Gianmarco

If you’ve eaten with us recently, you’ve already had his cooking. Gianmarco Abramo is our Head Chef, and he runs the kitchen day to day alongside Matthew.

He trained at Sketch, Joël Robuchon and Aquavit, then led the kitchens at The Rabbit and Cora Pearl. He’s also the one behind our seasonal specials, which change each week with what’s best from the boats in Rye Bay and the fields of the Marsh.
