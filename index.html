Happiness is a place

Sky and beach as far as the eye can see, a French brasserie by the sea and deeply comfortable beds, just behind the dunes.

Welcome

Come Gallivanting. The sand between your toes. Sky and beach as far as your eyes can see. Wonderful food made with love.

Deeply comfortable beds in beautifully thought-out bedrooms. When you stay with us time slows, creating more space to enjoy. Happiness is a place.

Our story

Stay

Rest assured. Twenty bedrooms, each one different: garden rooms with their own patch of green, Hamptons with marble bathrooms, and a snug cabin tucked away. Egyptian linen, feather and down, house cake at four and a proper turndown at night, with the beach a few steps from the door.

A perfect Gallivant day

Nothing to do, and all day to do it. Beach, cake, English wine, sunset in the dunes and dinner at Harry’s. Here’s how we’d spend it – though doing absolutely nothing is also allowed.

Ways to be happy

More than a place to sleep. Yoga in the dunes, events for kindred spirits, beach-house weddings and parties to remember.

Yoga

Walk in, float out. Daily classes with the nicest teachers – in our serene studio or ‘wild’ on the beach. Locals welcome.

 Weddings

Your private beach house. Our hotel becomes your private beach house by the sea, exclusively yours for you and your guests.

 What’s on

Curated events. Inspiring wellness events and holistic activities that anyone can book.

 Private parties

Parties to remember. Beautiful spaces, seasonal food and service that’s authentic and kind.

 Corporate

Businesses big and small. Board meetings, off-sites, product and fashion launches.

 Membership

A place you can join. Access to The Gallivant at all times, and a range of exclusive privileges.

A year at the Gallivant

Every season by the sea.

Spring

Fresh air and long lunches. Yoga in the dunes, the first lunches on the Terrace and quiet beaches before the summer crowds.
