Your private house by the sea

The offsite that doesn’t feel like an offsite. For a night or two, The Gallivant stops being a hotel and becomes your team’s private house by the sea, with a team of our own who’ll know everyone’s name by dinner.

Strategy in the morning. A walk on a deserted beach after lunch. Matthew Harris menus in the evening. Meetings here tend to finish sooner than planned, and the decisions are better for it.

  • Leadership and board retreats
  • Department offsites and team building
  • Product and brand launches

One night or two

Close enough to come for the day, and exactly why it’s worth staying. Here’s how a stay usually runs. Holly will shape it around your agenda.

One night

Arrive for the agenda, stay for dinner. Arrive in the morning, get through the agenda, then let the evening be dinner at Harry’s rather than the train home.

  1. MorningArrive, coffee and the first session
  2. LunchA long table at Harry’s

Day two

  1. MorningWild yoga or a beach walk, then breakfast
  2. Late morningA closing session before you head home

Two nights

A full day to fill as you choose. Two nights by the coast gives a team a whole day with no arriving or leaving in it: time to properly reset, away from the noise of the office.

The beach

A beach across the road, and nobody on it. The beach across the road is deserted for most of the year. Our wellness programme makes the most of it, and two of the very best English wine estates are close by.

Questions

Adding a Christmas party to your offsite, or planning one on its own? Ask Holly about our Christmas packages for teams.

Ask about Christmas
How many people can you host?
Groups of up to 14 can stay without taking the whole house. From 15 guests, most teams take exclusive use of all twenty bedrooms. We can host up to 30 overnight, with luxury cottages a short walk away.
Is it exclusive use?
Most of our corporate clients take over the whole house for complete privacy, so the bedrooms, the bar, Harry’s and the garden are yours alone. Smaller groups can stay without exclusive hire.
How far is it from London?
About 1 hour 15 minutes. The high-speed train from St Pancras reaches Ashford International in 38 minutes, and we arrange private transfers from the station. By car it’s around two hours from central London.
What meeting facilities do you have?
Light-filled event spaces that configure for workshops, presentations and board meetings, the 120 sq m Open Space studio, high-speed Wi-Fi and a full package of AV on site.
Can you cater for dietary needs?
Yes. Matthew Harris tailors every corporate menu, so let us know about any dietary needs when you enquire.
Can we see The Gallivant before we book?
Of course. Ask Holly about visiting before you confirm, and she’ll arrange it.
